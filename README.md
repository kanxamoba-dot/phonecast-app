# 📱 PhoneCast v2.0.0 — Android & iPhone Screen Mirroring for Windows PC

**PhoneCast** is a zero-lag, 1080p 60 FPS screen mirroring, multi-phone audio, and instant file-sharing solution for Windows 10/11 PCs — supporting both **Android** (Wi-Fi, USB Tethering, USB AOA without USB Debugging) and **iPhone / iPad** (built-in AirPlay).

🌐 **Official Website:** [https://phonecast-app.netlify.app](https://phonecast-app.netlify.app)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🍎 Built-In iPhone & iPad AirPlay (1080p 60 FPS):** Mirror any iPhone or iPad screen and audio straight to your Windows PC with zero extra apps required on iOS — just select **PhoneCast-iPhone** from Screen Mirroring.
- **🎮 Plug & Play Android Mirroring:** Hardware-accelerated 60 FPS H.264 streaming over Wi-Fi, USB Tethering, or USB AOA — works completely without enabling USB Debugging.
- **🎧 Simultaneous Multi-Phone Audio:** Connect multiple Android phones at the same time and hear them together with individual Mute/Unmute controls and automatic Bluetooth/earphone switching.
- **📤 Two-Way File & Clipboard Bridge:** Drag & drop files directly onto the PC window to send to connected phones, share from Android Gallery, and sync clipboard text automatically.
- **🔒 100% Local & Private:** All video, audio, clipboard text, and shared files travel strictly over your local Wi-Fi or USB cable. No data ever touches external cloud servers.
- **🔑 7-Day Free Evaluation:** Test every Android & iPhone feature free for 7 days on your Windows PC.

## 🚀 Quick Setup Guide

### 1. Windows PC Setup
1. Download **`PhoneCast_Setup.zip`** from the [Releases](https://github.com/kanxamoba-dot/phonecast-app/releases/latest) section.
2. Extract the `.zip` file and run **`PhoneCast_Setup.exe`** (if Windows SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**).
3. The installer automatically configures the signed USB AOA driver, Apple Bonjour Service (for iPhone AirPlay), and local firewall rules.

### 2. Android Connection
1. Download **`PhoneCast apk.zip`**, extract it, and install **`PhoneCast.apk`** on your Android phone (Android 7.0–16).
2. Connect your phone and PC to the same Wi-Fi/Hotspot, or plug in a USB cable (USB Tethering / USB AOA).
3. Open **PhoneCast** on your phone and tap **Start Mirroring**.

### 3. iPhone / iPad Connection (AirPlay)
1. Open **PhoneCast** on your Windows PC (AirPlay starts automatically).
2. Ensure your iPhone/iPad and Windows PC are on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. Open **Control Center → Screen Mirroring** on your iPhone and select **`PhoneCast-iPhone`**.
